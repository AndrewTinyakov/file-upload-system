The historical cliff does not identify a language advantage

The original Bash-era and first Node-era built browser bundles, measured sources and dependency locks are identical. Today's VM samples show active reclaim and paging, and the historical slow run had many more API page faults. Host contention is the strongest explanation, with the exact past cause unproven.

Keep Node, retain Bash as a diagnostic, and keep PR2 draft. Separate multipart concurrency from the single-upload queue in an isolated benchmark. In the low-rate WAN pair, all 12 uploads verified; multipart upload-stage mean was 9.81 s versus 8.54 s, with only two samples per side.
